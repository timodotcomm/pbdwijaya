Profile

A long track record in gas and water piping backed by a commitment to quality, safety and customer satisfaction.

Established in 1989

PB Dwijaya was established in 1989 and has since become a trusted partner of PT Perusahaan Gas Negara (Persero) Tbk, completing numerous gas pipeline projects.

In 2012, the company received recognition from PT PGN (Persero) Tbk SBU Region I for its contribution to gas pipeline network repairs.

PB Dwijaya has also worked with PT Aetra and PT Palyja on water pipeline projects in Jakarta.
